# Generate a Packing List
Nomadic uses your trip details to create a customized packing list for your hiking or backpacking trip. You must be signed in to generate and save a full packing list.

## Create a Packing List
To begin creating a packing list for your next adventure, follow the steps below:
1. Open Nomadic on your browser or mobile app.
2. Click the **New Trip** tab.
3. Select your trip details from the following drop-down menus:
   - Activity type
   - Trip duration
   - Time of year
   - Backpack size in liters
4. Click **Generate packing list**

Nomadic takes care of the second-guessing and endless researching of products behind the scenes, and creates an organized list of recommended gear we think best suits you for your trip. You can edit lists by manually adding or removing items, and download lists as PDFs to save on your computer or phone.
*AI-generated suggestions are designed to help you plan, but they should not replace your own judgment or local safety guidelines and regulations. Always check trail conditions and local weather reports before you go.
