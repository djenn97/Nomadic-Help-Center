# Nomadic
Nomadic helps solo hikers and backpackers alike plan and prepare for safer, more confident trips. Use Nomadic to generate an AI-powered packing list based on your trip details, download offline maps and its details for areas without service, and read trail and campsite reviews and features before you go.
Nomadic is available on both the web and mobile; however, some features, such as downloading offline maps, are only available on the free mobile app.
You can explore trails and preview packing lists without an account. To explore full features, including saving packing lists, downloading offline maps, and reading and writing trail reviews, just create a free account!

## Who Nomadic is for
Nomadic was inspired by and designed for people who:
- Travel solo or in small groups
- Seek structured and reliable data and suggestions about their trip so they can be as prepared as possible
- Are anywhere from first-timer or veteran day hikers, trekkers, or backpackers wanting to stay safe

## Key Features
- **AI-powered packing lists** customized to your trip details
- **Offline maps** to use in areas without cell phone service (*mobile only*)
- **Trail and campsite reviews** from other travelers
