# Getting Started
Start using Nomadic right away to explore trails and preview packing lists! To access all of its features, create a **free** account by following the steps below:

## Create an Account
1. Go to *nomadic.app* or download then open the Nomadic app on your mobile device.
2. Select **Sign up**.
3. Enter your email address and create a secure password.
4. Check your email inbox to verify and activate your Nomadic account.

Once you're logged in to your Nomadic account, you can:
- Generate and save AI-powered packing lists
- Download offline maps on the mobile app
- Read and write trail and campsite reviews
- Get notified for hazardous weather conditions in your trip's area

## Without an Account
We suggest creating a **free** account so you can access all of Nomadic's helpful features! Even without an account, you can:
- Search and browse for trails and campsites
- Preview a limited number of trail and campsite reviews
- Preview a limited packing list based on your trip details
